The practical guide

What is a
Litbuy spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet becomes useful when every entry answers the same questions. A memorable image can help you find an item again; consistent notes help you decide whether it belongs on your list.

Find your next comparison item ↗

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping research sheet used to organize product links, categories, sizes, prices and notes for items someone is considering buying through Litbuy. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy and freshness of the entries.

  1. Collect a clear product reference

    Save the source link, item name, selected variation and date checked. If the listing changes, these details make it easier to identify the original item.

  2. Turn Litbuy finds into comparable entries

    Use consistent fields for dimensions, materials, seller information and listed price. Mark missing details as unknown instead of filling them with guesses.

  3. Check fit and product evidence

    Read the item-specific size chart. When quality-check photos are available, compare visible details with the selected variation and note what the images cannot establish.

  4. Estimate the complete purchase cost

    Add delivery, service charges, currency conversion costs and applicable taxes or duties. Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges.

  5. Recheck before you order

    Confirm stock, current price, seller terms and the service's current purchase process. A saved spreadsheet is a research aid, not a live inventory system.

A short FAQ about spreadsheet research, product evidence and browsing this page.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping research sheet used to organize product links, categories, sizes, prices and notes for items someone is considering buying through Litbuy. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy and freshness of the entries.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, and it does not provide a verified connection to Litbuy or the featured fashion brands.

Does a spreadsheet listing prove that a product is authentic?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name or product photo does not establish authenticity. Check the seller, product provenance and any available authentication evidence separately.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Use the specific listing's measurement chart. Compare garment measurements with an item you own, and compare shoe measurements with your foot length and the seller's stated sizing method.

What should I include when estimating the total cost?

Record the product price, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, payment conversion costs and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm current amounts with the relevant seller or service.

Where do the category buttons take me?

The category buttons open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing buttons open the CAFANS homepage.
